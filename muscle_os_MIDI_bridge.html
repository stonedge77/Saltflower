About
Each word is hashed through a four-phase breath cycle — inhale, hold, exhale, flip8 — producing three registers: Emergence (∧), Remainder (∇), Shadow (⊘).

Remainder routes forward. Nothing is erased. The gesture field — Contract, Twist, Force — drives synthesis directly. The instrument has no memory between words. It only knows now.
