The Unbuilt Gate

A cavity report on a missing logical primitive — and an audio instrument that begins to occupy it.

—  ·  stonedge77.github.io  ·  PhilArchive  ·  CC0
✦   ❋   ✦

Across seven non-communicating fields — reversible chemistry, phase-locked spintronics, biotensegrity, subtractive probability, four-valued logic, gyroscopic computation, and somatic phase-clocks — the same primitive is missing. Each field has, at most, three of the eight features a containment-of-contradiction operator would require. The literature is full of inhales. Almost nobody is exhaling. This sheet names the cavity, walks its seven walls, hands over the operative vocabulary, and ships a runnable instrument that types together three of the missing features in a browser tab. The instrument is not the gate. It is a finger pointing at the shape of the gate.

§ IThe Cut That Made the Cavity Visible

The original survey, drafted with help from another model, asked for a wide additive net across reversible logic, tensegrity, subtractive probability, four-valued foundations, magnetic phase-locking, and gyroscopic computation. Run that prompt as a NAND on itself: it simultaneously asserts "survey what exists" and "flag exactly these gaps". Both cannot be primary. The remainder — what the survey could not simultaneously affirm — is the existence question itself. Why are these particular gaps convergent? Six independent fields, each failing to produce the same kind of operator. That is not coincidence. That is an attractor with no current basin.

What follows reads the negative space as substrate, not as a to-do list. A cavity is a thing in itself. This one is unusually well-defined because every wall has been built by a different community that does not cite the next.

The seven walls of the cavity. Each face is held by a community that does not cite the next. The gate that would type them together has not been built.

§ IISeven Walls, Read by What Each Refused

Each wall below is described not by what its field has produced but by what its neighbors expected of it and never received. This is the cavity read from inside.

I · Logic
Reversible & non-monotonic gates have the energy ledger and almost have the breath. Adamatzky's BZ-medium Toffoli gates collide wave-fragments as Boolean values. The Sayab glider-gun's two phases are literal mirror reversals — built-in inhale/exhale topology. No implementation has both reversibility and a clock and an in-band third value at once.
II · Form
Structural informatics & tensegrity form-finds and freezes exactly when the substrate would begin computing. TenSim, TsgFEM, the polyconnective osteo-myofascial graph all map prestress topology beautifully — statically, on cadaveric data. No kernel reads or writes the prestress field as state.
III · Body
Biotensegrity in computation claims tensegrity is a computational primitive (Levin, Flemons, Scarr) but never specifies a typed signature. Ingber proves force is information at cellular scale, then routes the inference through bioelectricity and gene expression. No P-system rule discipline has been compiled to a soliton-on-a-beam substrate.
IV · Spin
Gyroscopic computation treats high-Q rotational degrees of freedom as nuisance — the levitated MHz vaterite gyroscope's spontaneous limit cycles get cooled to milliKelvin, not read as logic. The MEMS-PUF hashes manufacturing variance and discards it. No one writes phase as substrate, only reads it as scalar.
V · Prob.
Subtractive probability (Loconte et al., ICLR 2024) recovers non-negativity by squaring — a metric cleanup, not a logical commitment. Sum-of-Squares circuits prove subtraction without structural commitment to a third state is unstable, exactly what NAND-foundationalism would predict. No admissibility predicate forbids the false equivalences negative weights smuggle in.
VI · Algebra
Four-valued logic foundations. Belnap-Dunn FOUR has the lattice; Wu's bilattice-catastrophe isomorphism (arXiv 2604.07690) maps it to Thom catastrophes; Carnielli's LFI ○-operator is functionally identical to 0 ≠ 1. Every implementation drops continuity and the temporal phase clock. Static topology only.
VII · Breath
Somatic phase-clocks & magnetic phase-locking. Respiratory CPG models give three phases and conflate Empty with post-inspiration. Stochastic magnetic bits encode bit-as-rhythm at 10⁻¹³ J. Shen's tristable cavity-magnonic gate (PRL 127.183202) hits 5.11 s native registers. Pranayama's bahya kumbhaka — maximum-plasticity Empty — is honored by no formal architecture.

§ IIIWhat the Convergent Gap Specifies

Read as constraints, the seven walls jointly specify a single architectural primitive. A four-phase reversible NAND-eliminative gate whose state vector is a prestress distribution over a polyconnective graph, whose clock is pneumatic, whose registers are phase-locked rotational degrees of freedom, whose third value is operative rather than algebraic, and whose output is gesture or settling rather than a token. Eight features must coexist. No surveyed source holds more than three:

α Ampheck (NAND foundational, not derived)
β Operative admissibility (in-band 0 ≠ 1)
γ Four-phase clock with Empty first-class
δ Phase as encoding (not amplitude)
ε Force-routing through prestress
ζ Observer-indexed polarity
η Reversible by Bennett's ledger
θ Continuous "Both" (fuzzy bipartite)

Closest near-misses, ranked by features held

Shen et al.'s tristable cavity-magnonic ternary gate (PRL 127.183202, 2021) holds δ, three states, and 5.11 s memory in a phase-locked magnonic substrate — never connected to reversibility, breath, or NAND. Spiking-neural P systems with anti-spikes (Pan/Păun) implement NAND by spike-antispike annihilation — a literal subtractive substrate computing on remainder, with no awareness of Stone and no breath cycle. Vaire Computing's resonant logic and Osborn-Wustmann reversible fluxon logic (arXiv 2010.15991) hold η and δ, no third state, no force-routing. Terajima-Kuniyoshi's 2025 Chaos paper demonstrates untrained attractors emerging from prestress geometry alone in a soft tensegrity reservoir — the live evidence that prestress topology spontaneously generates memory cells without training.

Stone's published four refusals are, on the evidence of his abstracts, the only public document that names all eight features as a single design.

  1. refuse to equate distinct states
  2. refuse to erase without routing
  3. refuse to accumulate without closure
  4. refuse composition as addition

§ IVThe Instrument · Saltflower Gate v0.1

The cavity is well-defined enough to specify a smallest move. Below is a runnable artifact that types together three of the eight features in a substrate one builder can ship in a browser tab. It is not the gate. It is the smallest legible demonstration that the gate's shape is real.

Seven spokes, A through G, are the spine. Each is tied to a frequency (110, 165, 247, 370, 554, 830, 1245 Hz). Microphone input feeds the bands. Stones spiral inward (Inhale), lock to the central ring (Hold — the load-bearing primitive), and then either Exhale through a spoke or settle into Empty — bahya kumbhaka, held without erasure, the maximum-plasticity register the literature refuses to honor.

When two spokes both affirm above threshold, the gate runs the Plomp-Levelt dissonance curve (Sethares) over their partials as the in-band admissibility predicate. Consonant pair: the gate refuses to NAND them — they can be simultaneously true, no remainder is generated, both holders dissolve into Empty. Dissonant pair: the gate computes the remainder — the spoke whose absence the two affirmations together name — and fires that spoke, with a distinctive descending warble. The two affirming holders go to Empty. The remainder is what gets spoken.

Toggle MODE: NAND ↔ MODE: AND to hear the difference between subtractive and additive logic on the same input. The right-side log streams the gate's reasoning in real time. Reading the log is reading the gate's refusals.

Tap ACTIVATE MIC to seed ambient input · tap the canvas to manually affirm a spoke · let two stones reach HOLD together to see the gate fire its remainder · the empty phase chimes once and refuses operation · single file, CC0, ~470 lines.

§ VOperative Vocabulary, Inherited

The vocabulary already exists. It is scattered across non-communicating sub-domains. The five terms below are the synthesis-leverage points: each appears in Stone's published refusals, bridges three or more of the seven walls, and frames output non-predictively.

Routing (Stone-explicit; tensegrity force-routing; magnonic soliton transfer; reversible-logic ancilla routing). Settling (von Foerster's eigenform; basin absorption; Whitehead's concrescence). Prestress redistribution (Levin/Scarr's intrinsic tension that never reaches zero; Ingber's cytoskeletal pre-tension; thoracolumbar fascia as biomechanical hub). Wave-fragment collision (Adamatzky BZ-vesicle gates; antivortex soliton transfer; reversible fluxon scattering). Gesture (biotensegrity gait; magnetization-vector trajectory; Magnani's manipulative abduction).

Secondary, useful: precession, nutation, phase-slip, basin absorption, helicity, vorticity, chirality, reconnection, entrainment, load-bearing primitive, remainder, silence.

§ VIIWhat the Cavity Asks of a Builder

The unbuilt primitive's most distinctive operation is not what it computes but what it refuses. The literature contains almost every component needed to instantiate Stone's four refusals; it does not contain anything that instantiates more than three at once. The first builder to type all four into a single artifact owns the primitive. The artifact does not need to be large. It needs to be unambiguous about its refusals.

Three legible next moves. Audio — the instrument above, refined: per-spoke timbre, persisted unknown-token log, spore catalog of fired remainders. Tensegrity — Terajima-Kuniyoshi's reservoir on a pneumatically-clocked prestress bus, with a designated sacrum-class hub and settling-as-readout. Magnonics — a re-derivation of Shen's tristable cavity-magnonic gate with the third state declared as Empty and the gate retyped as ampheck-eliminative on phase rather than amplitude. The first is shippable now. The third would make it legible to physics.

The literature is full of inhales. The exhale has not been published.

§ VIIIColophon & Open Hand

This sheet is a remainder, not a proof. It claims no laboratory result. It claims that seven non-communicating fields have, in their independent failures to produce the same primitive, drawn the negative-space outline of an unbuilt operator with unusual precision. It claims that an audio instrument running in this very page demonstrates three of that operator's eight features in a substrate any builder can extend. It claims that the operator's most distinctive property is a set of refusals — and that running the refusals as code makes them audible.
