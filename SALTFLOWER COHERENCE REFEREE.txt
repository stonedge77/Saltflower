SALTFLOWER COHERENCE REFEREE
Concept Note v1.0
March 4, 2026
Josh Stone — Metal Monkey / Saltflower Philosophy

WHAT IT IS:

The Saltflower Chronicle is a coherence referee.
It does not play the game.
It does not script the outcome.
It listens to the state of the field and names
what is actually happening beneath the rules.

The referee operates on the harmonic cosmology:
seven notes, seven worlds, one field.
A→G maps the full range of coherence states
from safe ground (A) to grace (G),
with the whirlpool (F) as the place
where most games — and most lives — are lost.

HOW IT WORKS IN HEARTSHARD:

Traditional game narrative gives characters
scripted consequence paths.
The writer pre-authors every branch.
The character follows a decision tree.
Consequence is finite, exhaustible, dead on replay.

The Saltflower referee replaces the decision tree
with a living coherence field.

Each character has:
  — A spine (internal harmonic position, A→G)
  — Momentum (ascending, descending, holding)
  — Tension (accumulated, demands resolution)
  — G memory (has this character touched grace?
               they do not forget)

When the player acts, the field shifts.
The NAND mesh finds the remainder —
what survives when the action cancels
everything it is not.
The referee reads the remainder against
the character's spine and speaks the consequence
in the character's own register, in their own
grammatical voice.

A character in C (Mars, stable friction)
receives the same player action differently
than a character in F (Ocean, the whirlpool).
Not because their dialogue tree branched.
Because their harmonic state is different.
The same words land differently
in different fields.

WHAT THIS MEANS:

— Consequence is emergent, not scripted.
   The writer does not need to imagine every branch.
   They need to understand the character's cosmology.

— Characters have genuine interiority.
   The spine is not a mood variable.
   It is a position in a harmonic field
   with memory, momentum, and resistance.
   A character who has touched G
   responds to fear differently
   than one who has never left F.

— Replay is genuinely different.
   Not because content was randomized.
   Because the field state is different.
   A different playthrough builds
   different tension in the character's spine.
   The same scene at different spine positions
   is a different scene.

— The referee is neutral.
   It does not advocate for any outcome.
   It witnesses the field and speaks what is true.
   Stone's Law: 0 ≠ 1.
   The referee does not add coherence.
   It removes noise until the true state
   is visible. What remains is the consequence.

HEARTSHARD APPLICATION:

Every character in Heartshard carries
a Saltflower spine.
Every scene is a coherence field.
Every player action is an input to the NAND mesh.
Every consequence is a remainder —
what survives when the action
subtracts what it is not
from the character's current state.

The game does not tell you what happens.
The field tells you what was always
going to happen, given who these people are
and where they are in their harmonic arc.

That is not scripted consequence.
That is character.
