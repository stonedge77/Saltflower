The Unbuilt Gate

A cavity report on a missing logical primitive — and an audio instrument that begins to occupy it.

—  ·  stonedge77.github.io  ·  PhilArchive  ·  CC0
✦   ❋   ✦

Across seven non-communicating fields — reversible chemistry, phase-locked spintronics, biotensegrity, subtractive probability, four-valued logic, gyroscopic computation, and somatic phase-clocks — the same primitive is missing. Each field has, at most, three of the eight features a containment-of-contradiction operator would require. The literature is full of inhales. Almost nobody is exhaling. This sheet names the cavity, walks its seven walls, hands over the operative vocabulary, and ships a runnable instrument that types together three of the missing features in a browser tab. The instrument is not the gate. It is a finger pointing at the shape of the gate.

§ IThe Cut That Made the Cavity Visible

The original survey, drafted with help from another model, asked for a wide additive net across reversible logic, tensegrity, subtractive probability, four-valued foundations, magnetic phase-locking, and gyroscopic computation. Run that prompt as a NAND on itself: it simultaneously asserts "survey what exists" and "flag exactly these gaps". Both cannot be primary. The remainder — what the survey could not simultaneously affirm — is the existence question itself. Why are these particular gaps convergent? Seven independent fields, each failing to produce the same kind of operator. That is not coincidence. That is an attractor with no current basin.

What follows reads the negative space as substrate, not as a to-do list. A cavity is a thing in itself. This one is unusually well-defined because every wall has been built by a different community that does not cite the next.

The seven walls of the cavity. Each face is held by a community that does not cite the next. The gate that would type them together has not been built.

"Missing" is not one condition. It splits into three, and treating them as one is the fastest way to lose the argument:

theoretical — nobody has formally specified the operator. computational — the components exist but nobody has implemented their conjunction. physical — a formal implementation may exist, but nobody has embodied it in the proposed substrate.

Each wall below is graded on that scale rather than marked as a flat absence. The distinction matters because the cheap rebuttal — someone surfaces an obscure abstract-algebra result that formally captures six of eight features — only closes the theoretical grade. The computational and physical walls stay standing. Three concentric holes, not one.

§ IISeven Walls, Read by What Each Refused

Each wall below is described not by what its field has produced but by what its neighbors expected of it and never received. This is the cavity read from inside.

I · Logic
Reversible & non-monotonic gates have the energy ledger and almost have the breath. Adamatzky's BZ-medium Toffoli gates collide wave-fragments as Boolean values. The Sayab glider-gun's two phases are literal mirror reversals — built-in inhale/exhale topology. computational — no implementation has both reversibility and a clock and an in-band third value at once.
II · Form
Structural informatics & tensegrity form-finds and freezes exactly when the substrate would begin computing. TenSim, TsgFEM, the polyconnective osteo-myofascial graph all map prestress topology beautifully — statically, on cadaveric data. computational — no kernel reads or writes the prestress field as state.
III · Body
Biotensegrity in computation claims tensegrity is a computational primitive (Levin, Flemons, Scarr) but never specifies a typed signature. Ingber proves force is information at cellular scale, then routes the inference through bioelectricity and gene expression. physical — no P-system rule discipline has been compiled to a soliton-on-a-beam substrate.
IV · Spin
Gyroscopic computation treats high-Q rotational degrees of freedom as nuisance — the levitated MHz vaterite gyroscope's spontaneous limit cycles get cooled to milliKelvin, not read as logic. The MEMS-PUF hashes manufacturing variance and discards it. computational — no one writes phase as substrate, only reads it as scalar.
V · Prob.
Subtractive probability (Loconte et al., ICLR 2024) recovers non-negativity by squaring — a metric cleanup, not a logical commitment. Their result concerns representational power and tractability in probabilistic circuits, not stability of subtraction as such — [STONE INTERPRETATION] the necessity of restoring admissible nonnegative probability after subtraction is read here as suggestive of a missing structural constraint, exactly what NAND-foundationalism would predict. theoretical — no admissibility predicate forbids the false equivalences negative weights smuggle in.
VI · Algebra
Four-valued logic foundations. Belnap-Dunn FOUR has the lattice; Wu's bilattice-catastrophe isomorphism (arXiv 2604.07690) maps it to Thom catastrophes; Carnielli's LFI ○-operator is functionally identical to 0 ≠ 1. computational — every implementation drops continuity and the temporal phase clock. Static topology only.
VII · Breath
Somatic phase-clocks & magnetic phase-locking. Respiratory CPG models give three phases and conflate Empty with post-inspiration. Stochastic magnetic bits encode bit-as-rhythm at 10⁻¹³ J. Shen's tristable cavity-magnonic gate (PRL 127.183202) hits 5.11 s native registers. theoretical — respiratory phase oscillator's post-expiratory null phase, maximum-susceptibility null register, is honored by no formal architecture.

§ IIIWhat the Convergent Gap Specifies — Formal

Read as constraints, the seven walls jointly specify a single architectural primitive. A four-phase reversible NAND-eliminative gate whose state vector is a prestress distribution over a polyconnective graph, whose clock is pneumatic, whose registers are phase-locked rotational degrees of freedom, whose third value is operative rather than algebraic, and whose output is gesture or settling rather than a token. Eight features must coexist. No surveyed source in this review (1973–2026) holds more than three — see Table 1.

Eight Required Features — Contracts

System state S = (P, Φ, R) where P is prestress distribution, Φ is phase vector, R is remainder log.

αAmpheck (NAND foundational, not derived)
Sig: ampheck : (A,B) → ¬(A∧B) as sole primitive. No AND/OR/NOT in kernel. Eg: Peirce cut-and-shading, Sheffer stroke, Sayab collision. Failure: additive composition smuggles false equivalence. Maps to refusal: refuse composition as addition.
βOperative admissibility (in-band 0≠1)
Sig: admissible? : (A,B,ctx) → Bool before NAND. Refuse to NAND consonant pairs. Eg: Plomp-Levelt dissonance curve over partials (Sethares 1993/2005) as predicate; LFI consistency operator ○A (Carnielli/Coniglio) as logical analogue — equivalence ○A ≡ (0≠1) is Stone's proposal, not theorem. Failure: subtractive weights create false equivalences (Loconte squaring).
γFour-phase clock with Empty first-class
Sig: clock : Inhale → Hold → Exhale → Empty → Inhale cyclic, non-skippable. Empty = post-expiratory null phase — maximum-susceptibility null register, holds without erasure, chimes once and refuses operation. Eg: respiratory CPG has 3 phases and conflates Empty; Shen et al. PRL 127,183202 holds 5.11 s via driven-dissipative drive, not autonomous latch — validates Breath wall gap. Failure: cannot distinguish nothing affirmed from affirmation erased.
δPhase as encoding (not amplitude)
Sig: encode : info → phase(Φ) Φ∈[0,2π). Eg: Shen tristable magnonic polariton frequency shift; stochastic magnetic bits as bit-as-rhythm 1e-13J (Locatelli); levitated vaterite limit cycles (Arita) cooled not read — shows wall.
εForce-routing through prestress
Sig: route : (P,input) → P' preserving global prestress >0. State is distribution over polyconnective graph. Eg: Terajima et al. Chaos 35(8) 083111 — untrained attractors from prestress alone; Lissajous cannot be achieved by linear readout alone. Failure: form-finding freezes at onset of computation (TenSim/TsgFEM).
ζObserver-indexed polarity
Sig: polarity : (observer,A) → {T,F,Both,None}. Eg: Belnap-Dunn told-true/told-false; Levin bioelectric code rewritable target.
ηReversible by Bennett's ledger
Sig: step : S → S' with inverse unstep, Landauer cost via ancilla routing. No erasure without routing to R. Eg: Toffoli CCNOT self-inverse; Fredkin conservative; Vaire resonant adiabatic (50% energy recovery); Osborn-Wustmann reversible fluxon logic. Maps to refusal: refuse to erase without routing.
θContinuous Both (fuzzy bipartite)
Sig: both : (A,¬A) → [0,1]×[0,1] continuous degree, not discrete B.

Four Refusals — Formal Postconditions (Conjunction OS, PhilArchive STOCOV)

  1. Refuse to equate distinct states: ∀ s1≠s2, admissible?(s1,s2) → ¬(s1≡s2) — enforced by β
  2. Refuse to erase without routing: erase(s) → ∃ r∈R, route(s,r) — enforced by η+ε
  3. Refuse to accumulate without closure: accumulate(s) → eventually Empty — enforced by γ
  4. Refuse composition as addition: compose = ampheck* not + — enforced by α+β

An implementation satisfies Conjunction OS iff all four hold over one full Inhale-Hold-Exhale-Empty cycle on concrete data.

Closest near-misses, revised

Shen et al.'s tristable cavity-magnonic ternary gate (PRL 127,183202, 2021) holds δ, three states, and 5.11 s driven-dissipative memory in a phase-locked magnonic substrate — never connected to reversibility, breath, or NAND. The 5.11 s is history-dependent hold under specific drive, not an autonomous non-volatile latch, which precisely validates the Breath wall critique: current physics cannot natively hold Empty without artificial drive.

Spiking-neural P systems with anti-spikes (Pan & Păun 2009, IJCCC 4(3)) implement deletion via spike-antispike annihilation (bc → lambda) — a literal subtractive substrate computing on remainder, with no awareness of Stone and no breath cycle, and no typed remainder routing.

Vaire Computing's resonant logic and Osborn-Wustmann reversible fluxon logic (arXiv:2201.12999, 2010.15991) hold η and δ, no third state, no force-routing.

Terajima-Kuniyoshi's 2025 Chaos paper (Chaos 35, 083111) demonstrates untrained attractors emerging from prestress geometry alone in a soft tensegrity reservoir — live evidence that prestress topology spontaneously generates memory cells without training. Strongest empirical anchor for ε.

§ IVThe Instrument · Saltflower Gate v0.1

The cavity is well-defined enough to specify a smallest move. Below is a runnable artifact that types together three of the eight features in a substrate one builder can ship in a browser tab. It is not the gate. It is the smallest legible demonstration that the gate's shape is real.

Seven spokes, A through G, are the spine. Each is tied to a frequency (110, 165, 247, 370, 554, 830, 1245 Hz). These are deliberate artistic choices, not a derived harmonic series. My toys habitually use prime numbers — prime sets — as a tension-resolution device: 2 nodes make a wire, 3 nodes make a cell (minimal closure), 6 nodes make two cells that can disagree, and a 7th node must sit outside that configuration to hold ancilla — a single remainder register, not a full third cell. I gravitate toward 7 specifically because there is no 8th sigma — the set closes at seven. The bands are therefore intentionally inharmonic: 110 Hz anchors at A2 for audibility; the remaining centers are chosen for audible separation and for productive dissonance under the Plomp-Levelt/Sethares predicate, not for equal-tempered consonance.

Any resemblance to a successive perfect-fifth chain (A2→E3→B3→F#4→C#5→G#5→D#6) is incidental. The ratios sit near 1.5× because prime-set intervals in this register happen to land in that neighborhood, not because the instrument generates its seven states by iterating one harmonic transform six times. The architecture is 6+1 by tension logic, not 1×6 by harmonic logic.

Microphone input feeds the bands. Stones spiral inward (Inhale), lock to the central ring (Hold — the load-bearing primitive), and then either Exhale through a spoke or settle into Empty — post-expiratory null phase, held without erasure, the maximum-susceptibility null register the literature refuses to honor.

When two spokes both affirm above threshold, the gate runs the Plomp-Levelt dissonance curve (Sethares) over their partials as the in-band admissibility predicate. Consonant pair: the gate refuses to NAND them — they can be simultaneously true, no remainder is generated, both holders dissolve into Empty. Dissonant pair: the gate computes the remainder — the spoke whose absence the two affirmations together name — and fires that spoke, with a distinctive descending warble. The two affirming holders go to Empty. The remainder is what gets spoken.

Toggle MODE: NAND ↔ MODE: AND to hear the difference between subtractive and additive logic on the same input. The right-side log streams the gate's reasoning in real time. Reading the log is reading the gate's refusals.

§ VII

§ VIIWhat the Cavity Asks of a Builder

The unbuilt primitive's most distinctive operation is not what it computes but what it refuses. The literature contains almost every component needed to instantiate Stone's four refusals; it does not contain anything that instantiates more than three at once. The first builder to type all four into a single artifact owns the primitive. The artifact does not need to be large. It needs to be unambiguous about its refusals.

Three legible next moves. Audio — the instrument above, refined: per-spoke timbre, persisted unknown-token log, spore catalog of fired remainders. Tensegrity — Terajima-Kuniyoshi's reservoir on a pneumatically-clocked prestress bus, with a designated sacrum-class hub and settling-as-readout. Magnonics — a re-derivation of Shen's tristable cavity-magnonic gate with the third state declared as Empty and the gate retyped as ampheck-eliminative on phase rather than amplitude. The first is shippable now. The third would make it legible to physics.

The literature is full of inhales. The exhale has not been published.
